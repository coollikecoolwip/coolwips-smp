04 / Player verification

LINK YOUR
ACCOUNT.

CoolWips SMP requires Minecraft–Discord account linking. Follow these instructions for either Java or Bedrock Edition.

01

Join Minecraft first

Connect to CoolWips SMP from Java or Bedrock. If your account isn't linked yet, the server will provide a unique four-letter/number code (for example, A7K9).

02

Verify in Discord

Join the CoolWips Discord, open #link-account, click Verify, enter your code, and press Submit.

03

Connect again

After the bot says Linked successfully, reconnect to the Minecraft server. Already linked? You can join normally.

Private verification

Linking codes last 10 minutes. Enter them only through the Verify button in Discord. Never give your account password to anyone. Need to remove your link? Use Unlink account in the same channel.

05 / Bedrock console setup

JOIN ON
CONSOLE.

You may need a third-party connector to join a custom Bedrock server on Xbox, PlayStation, or Switch. Availability varies by console, Minecraft version, and network.

01

Use a console connector

Open BedrockTogether or a similar Bedrock console connector app on your phone. Your phone and console should be on the same Wi-Fi.

02

Enter the server details

In your connector app, enter the Bedrock IP and port shown here. Start or run the connection.

03

Open Minecraft on console

Start Minecraft Bedrock Edition on Xbox, PlayStation, or Nintendo Switch.

04

Join the session

Open Friends or the relevant Servers / LAN section. Look for the session created by the connector and select it. If Minecraft requests account linking, follow the Discord steps above.

BEDROCK SERVER DETAILS

IP ADDRESS

domination-ashlee.tun.ply.gg

PORT

19132

Use the same IP and port in the connector app. This does not require you to enter a Minecraft or Microsoft password on this website.

If the server doesn't show up

Restart Minecraft and the connection app. Make sure your phone and console are on the same Wi-Fi, not separate guest networks. Restart the connector session and check its current instructions if you still can't see the server.

01

Joining & Setup

7 QUESTIONS
Can I join on Java or Bedrock?

Yes. Java players use tw-observers.tun.ply.gg. Bedrock players use domination-ashlee.tun.ply.gg on port 19132.

Can Java and Bedrock players play together?

Yes. CoolWips SMP supports crossplay between Java and Bedrock editions.

How do I join on Xbox, PlayStation, or Switch?

Use a console connection method such as BedrockTogether to join the custom Bedrock server. Results vary by platform and network. Read the console guide.

Do I need Discord to play?

Yes. CoolWips SMP requires Minecraft–Discord account linking before joining. View the linking instructions.

How do I link my Minecraft account?

Connect to CoolWips SMP, get your four-character code, then click Verify in Discord's #link-account channel and submit the code. Reconnect when linked.

What if my verification code expires?

Linking codes expire after 10 minutes. Reconnect to obtain a new code and complete the verification in Discord.

Why can't I connect to the server?

Check your edition's address, port 19132 for Bedrock, and Discord linking. For console, confirm the connector and phone are on the same Wi-Fi as the console. If problems persist, contact staff.

02

Gameplay & Economy

8 QUESTIONS
What type of server is CoolWips SMP?

Anarchy-style Minecraft survival with raiding, PvP, alliances, land claims, and a player economy.

Are raiding, griefing, and stealing allowed?

Yes. These activities, traps, and destroying builds are permitted as in-game gameplay. Real-life threats and attacks on the server are not.

Are hacks or cheats allowed?

The published server rules allow in-game cheating and hacking. Deliberately crashing the server, account theft, and attacks on hosting are prohibited.

Is PvP and spawn killing allowed?

Yes. Both PvP and spawn killing are allowed under the server's anarchy-style gameplay rules.

How do I protect my base?

Use the land-claim system, secure your belongings, and think carefully before sharing your base location. Read the full server rules.

Will staff return stolen items or restore griefed builds?

No. Staff do not refund or roll back items or builds lost to gameplay that the rules permit.

Can players create alliances or teams?

Yes. Alliances, rivalries, betrayal, and cooperation are part of the experience.

Does CoolWips SMP have an economy?

Yes. Players can trade, farm resources, and grow their wealth. Market prices can change.

03

Discord, Safety & Support

9 QUESTIONS
Where do I find server updates?

Join the CoolWips Discord for announcements and community discussions.

How do I contact staff?

Ask for help through Discord, and see administrator and moderator roles in the staff section.

Where do I report a waypoint problem?

Open the Waypoint Report channel in Discord. Describe the waypoint and location so staff can review it.

Where do I report a land claim problem?

Use the Claim Report channel in Discord. Include the area or coordinates and an explanation. View both report links.

How do I report rule-breaking?

Report doxxing, account theft, scams, real-life threats, or intentional server attacks privately to staff. Do not publicly repost private information.

Are doxxing or tracking links allowed?

No. Trying to find or expose private information, tracking people, IP grabbers, and leaking personal information are prohibited.

Can I unlink my Minecraft and Discord accounts?

Yes. Use Unlink account in the Discord #link-account channel. You may need to link again before joining.

What happens if someone breaks the rules?

Staff may issue warnings, mutes, kicks, or bans. Doxxing attempts, account theft, and server attacks may result in a permanent ban.

Is this the official CoolWips website?

Yes. It contains server connection details, staff information, rules, account-linking help, and joining instructions.
